# EP-001 — prompts prontos para colar (Gemini app / Google Flow, Veo 3.1)
Um prompt por cena (o Veo gera no máximo 8 s por vez). Em cada cena, anexe as imagens de `personagens/` indicadas. Formato 9:16.
**Evitar (negative prompt):** subtitles, captions, on-screen text, letters, watermark, logo, realistic animals, photorealistic, anime style, scary, dark lighting, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed paws, mismatched eyes, changed clothes, missing bow tie, missing bandana, adult proportions, English speech

## Cena 1 — usar 8 s · anexar: tico.jpg

```
Premium 3D animated children's film, cinematic, soft detailed fur, rounded cute shapes, big expressive glossy eyes, vibrant warm colors, vertical 9:16.
Setting: the garden of Vila Bambulim on a sunny morning — a big round apple tree full of shiny red apples, a red-and-white checkered picnic blanket with a small wicker basket under the tree, colorful flower beds, a low wooden fence, cute rounded cottages with pastel roofs and chimneys on soft green hills in the background, butterflies, floating dandelion seeds, golden morning sunlight.
Character: Tico, a chubby little brown bear cub with caramel-brown fluffy fur, round ears, light tan muzzle, big brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, wearing olive-green overalls with wooden buttons, a cream t-shirt and denim-blue sneakers.
Action: The camera glides down from the sky over the colorful cottages into the garden. Tico stands on tiptoes under the apple tree, stretching his paws toward the biggest apple on a low branch. He wobbles, plucks it with a little "pop", and lifts it high in the sunlight, eyes sparkling with joy.
Dialogue (Brazilian Portuguese, Tico, soft, slightly deep, slow and cuddly little-bear voice): "Uau! A maçã mais bonita da Vila Bambulim!"
Sound: cheerful light pizzicato and xylophone music, birds chirping, a soft "pop" when the apple comes off.
No subtitles, no on-screen text.
Keep the characters exactly as in the reference images.
```
